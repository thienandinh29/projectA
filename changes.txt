+# Financial-headline eligibility — development policy v1
+
+This policy is for today's development pilot, not a frozen final-test protocol.
+Review the exact saved headline version without model sentiment predictions or
+subsequent market returns. Use the source item only to establish headline provenance.
+Record a human reviewer identifier and a short reason for every judgment.
+
+Assess these three questions independently, using `yes`, `no`, or `uncertain`:
+
+1. **English:** Is the headline understandable English? Proper names, ticker
+   symbols and quoted foreign words do not by themselves disqualify it. For
+   mixed languages, garbled text or insufficient context, choose uncertain.
+2. **Financially relevant:** Does the headline concern markets, businesses,
+   corporate financial activity, economic indicators, public finances, or
+   economic policy? General entertainment/sports/lifestyle alone is no. A clear
+   financial/economic consequence qualifies; mere presence of a company name
+   does not guarantee relevance. Borderline cases are uncertain.
+3. **Genuine headline:** Is it a meaningful news headline supplied by the feed
+   or headline API? URL slugs, navigation text, empty placeholders and synthetic
+   titles constructed from filing identities are no. An authentic question,
+   opinion headline or older headline can qualify; age alone is not exclusion.
+
+All three yes means included. Any no means excluded. Otherwise the version
+remains uncertain and needs adjudication. Missing reviews remain pending.
+These judgments do not assign positive/neutral/negative sentiment. Mixed sentiment
+and entity-dependent sentiment will be handled in the separate annotation guide.
+
+Do not exclude a headline just because it is repeated or appears difficult for
+FinBERT. Preserve duplicates and revisions for the experiment's grouping controls.
+Keep original source/observation/storage times; eligibility does not change them.
+
+Corrections append a new decision referring to the old decision ID. The tool
+records actual decision availability at save time, conservatively later than the
+human reading time. It accepts no backdated availability. A policy edit changes
+its hash and requires new reviews under the new policy. Historical selections use
+only data and decisions strictly before their cutoff. Today's reviews therefore
+cannot be asserted as information available at an older historical cutoff.
+
+Single-annotator judgments remain a limitation. Document uncertain cases and seek
+independent review of a subset before freezing the final protocol.

+# Development sentiment annotation guide — draft v1
+
+Status: proposed for human review, not approved or frozen. Created October 6,
+2026. No sentiment labels have been assigned under this guide. Eligibility uses
+the separate, unchanged `headline-eligibility-dev-v1.md` policy.
+
+## What we are labeling
+
+Proposed target: the favorable, unfavorable or non-directional financial meaning
+expressed by the exact headline about its principal financial subject. This is
+a text interpretation task, not a prediction of returns or a measurement of the
+whole economy's health. Confirm this target before recording pilot labels.
+
+Identify the subject first: a named company/security, a market, households,
+public finances or an economic indicator. Use the subject and direction actually
+expressed in the headline. Do not silently switch from existing asset holders to
+prospective buyers, or from borrowers to lenders, to obtain a preferred label.
+If two perspectives compete and the headline supplies no clear principal
+perspective, flag it for review rather than impose one.
+
+## Label definitions
+
+| Label | Rule | Invented teaching example |
+| --- | --- | --- |
+| Positive | A clearly favorable financial development or outlook for the identified subject | Company A reports rising profit and raises its outlook |
+| Negative | A clearly unfavorable financial development or outlook for that subject | Company A reports falling profit and cuts its outlook |
+| Neutral | The headline states financial information without a clear favorable or unfavorable direction | Company A will publish its results on Friday |
+
+These examples are teaching material, not records in our dataset. Words such as
+stock, investor, dollars or inflation establish topic context; they do not alone
+determine polarity. A factual headline can still express positive or negative
+financial meaning. Neutral does not mean uncertain, difficult, false or irrelevant.
+
+## Difficult cases
+
+- Mixed: record both directions and their subjects. Assign a three-class label
+  only when the headline establishes a clear dominant financial message; otherwise
+  leave the label unresolved pending human adjudication. Do not automatically
+  map mixed sentiment to neutral.
+- Entity-dependent: identify each entity and explain the competing implications.
+  Do not assume all parties benefit or lose together.
+- Macroeconomic policy/indicators: rate hikes, inflation changes, tariffs and
+  employment numbers do not have a universal polarity. Use explicit evaluation,
+  comparison or consequences in the headline. Flag unresolved perspectives.
+- Forecasts, questions and quotations: label the expressed outlook when clear;
+  retain a flag that it is a forecast, question or attributed opinion. Do not
+  verify the claim by using later outcomes.
+- Uncertain interpretation: leave the sentiment field empty and record the
+  reason. This is an annotation status, not a fourth model class or neutral label.
+
+Retain every difficult item in the review inventory. Unresolved labels cannot
+enter scored three-class results yet; report their count and characteristics.
+Before final testing, freeze adjudication and reporting rules so difficult cases
+cannot be removed selectively after observing model errors.
+
+## What the annotator may see
+
+Read the exact stored title and its minimal source/version identifier. Do not
+use article bodies, later news, realized market prices, FinBERT probabilities or
+another annotator's decision to construct an independent headline-only judgment.
+Vocabulary help may explain a word without supplying its label; record such help.
+Today's assisted learning exercise is not blinded independent annotation.
+
+## Proposed pilot record and timing
+
+Record version ID, content hash, guide ID and guide hash, human annotator ID,
+financial subject/perspective, positive/negative/neutral or unresolved status,
+reason, and mixed/entity-dependent/forecast flags. Bind each decision to its exact
+input; a changed headline requires its own review. Append revisions referencing
+the previous decision rather than overwriting it.
+
+The release time of a persisted human judgment must be recorded in UTC, separately
+from publication, observation and DuckDB data availability. Never invent historical
+labels or reuse a source publication timestamp as label availability. A reporting
+cutoff must exclude judgments that were not yet released, including later revisions.
+
+This richer record and its durable release mechanism are requirements for the
+next sentiment-ledger implementation, not existing functionality. The legacy
+`SentimentLabel` schema supports three classes and basic provenance, but does not
+implement this review/adjudication ledger. The separate eligibility ledger is
+not a sentiment ledger. Verify durable annotation availability before final freeze.

+# Development sentiment ledger
+
+`research/annotations.py` and `scripts/review_sentiment.py` record human judgments
+separately from collection, operational deduplication and eligibility. Use the
+authoritative Docker snapshot/selection; the host's legacy DuckDB is not input.
+The SQLite annotation file resides in the same external research volume and must
+be backed up together with snapshots and the eligibility/session ledgers.
+
+## Workflow
+
+Rebuild `research-tools` after changing code. The preparation command verifies
+the snapshot content/provenance, eligibility-selection hashes and the ability to
+reproduce inclusion from saved decisions. It writes a new blank JSON template
+containing exact displayed titles and snapshot/selection/guide hashes.
+
+```powershell
+docker compose --profile tools build research-tools
+docker compose --profile tools run --rm --no-deps research-tools -m scripts.review_sentiment prepare --snapshot-dir /app/data/research/session01-20261006-snapshot --eligibility-selection /app/data/research/eligibility-batch08-reviewed-20261006 --guide-file /app/docs/sentiment-annotation-dev-v1.md --limit 3 --output /app/data/research/sentiment-practice-template.json
+```
+
+The default ordering is availability, observation and version ID. `--version-id`
+may select an explicit teaching subset, but requires a disclosed `--selection-reason`.
+Teaching subsets are not representative samples for accuracy estimates. Preparation
+generates zero judgments. Complete every field in `annotations` using actual human
+answers and retain the `inputs`/hashes. No caller-provided timestamps are accepted.
+
+```powershell
+docker compose --profile tools run --rm --no-deps research-tools -m scripts.review_sentiment record --snapshot-dir /app/data/research/session01-20261006-snapshot --eligibility-selection /app/data/research/eligibility-batch08-reviewed-20261006 --guide-file /app/docs/sentiment-annotation-dev-v1.md --reviews /app/data/research/sentiment-practice-completed.json --annotator project-owner
+```
+
+Use a new file for a correction with `supersedes_decision_id` set to the current
+decision in that annotator/version/guide chain. Another annotator starts their own
+chain; their answer cannot silently supersede yours. Changed guide bytes produce
+a new guide hash and do not inherit old labels.
+
+## Commit, interruption and recovery
+
+One transaction persists the whole validated batch. Only after it commits does
+the tool sample `label_available_at` and append separate immutable release markers.
+The timestamp confirms persisted content, not atomic publication to all clients
+at that instant; markers are committed separately. Selection requires a marker.
+All SQLite writes use FULL synchronous mode, subject to filesystem/host guarantees.
+
+A failure before the first commit rolls back the batch. A failure during sealing
+leaves committed judgments visible in history but unusable for selection. The
+error tells the operator to recover, not resubmit. Recovering does not replace or
+backdate the contents; it records current availability and `release_kind=recovery`.
+Clock regression fails instead of inventing a release time. A pending judgment
+must be recovered before adding a revision in its chain.
+
+```powershell
+docker compose --profile tools run --rm --no-deps research-tools -m scripts.review_sentiment recover
+```
+
+The ledger tables prevent normal SQL update/delete operations. This is audit
+protection against accidental edits, not security against an administrator who
+can change a database file/schema. Keep backups and hashed exports.
+
+## Selection and limits
+
+The `select` action requires an explicit annotator and a timezone-aware past cutoff:
+
+```powershell
+docker compose --profile tools run --rm --no-deps research-tools -m scripts.review_sentiment select --snapshot-dir /app/data/research/session01-20261006-snapshot --eligibility-selection /app/data/research/eligibility-batch08-reviewed-20261006 --guide-file /app/docs/sentiment-annotation-dev-v1.md --annotator project-owner --cutoff 2026-10-07T00:00:00Z --output /app/data/research/sentiment-before-review-example
+```
+
+That example cutoff cannot include labels recorded later. Choose the actual desired
+cutoff and a fresh output path for each selection. Eligibility is also replayed
+from archived decisions at that cutoff; a later eligibility review cannot supply
+earlier membership. Only archived decisions are available, not future updates to
+the live eligibility ledger. Sealed data and judgments must precede the cutoff
+strictly; later corrections do not change earlier selections. A latest
+unresolved judgment removes that version from three-class label output, while its
+full released history and unresolved status remain in the report. Unsealed revisions
+do not replace earlier released decisions. Labels are exported in the legacy
+`SentimentLabel` format; no model fitting, prediction or scoring occurs here.
+
+Multi-annotator adjudication, agreement statistics, representative pilot sampling,
+final-test rules, prediction timestamps and the full evaluation harness remain open.
+The existing eligibility ledger samples its timestamp before its batch commit;
+saved selections used cutoffs after successful returns, but its generic availability
+contract needs hardening before final freeze. This annotation ledger does not alter
+or relabel existing eligibility history.

+class Annotation(BaseModel):

+    content_hash: str
+    subject: str
+    status: Literal['labeled', 'unresolved']
+    sentiment: Literal['positive', 'negative', 'neutral'] | None
+    reason: str

+        if self.mixed and self.sentiment == 'neutral':
+            raise ValueError('Mixed directions are not neutral; resolve the dominant direction or leave unresolved')
+        if self.assisted and not self.assistance_note.strip():
+            raise ValueError('Assisted judgments must disclose the assistance')
+        if not self.assisted and self.assistance_note.strip():
+            raise ValueError('Assistance notes require assisted=true')

+def load_guide(path):
+    raw = Path(path).read_bytes()
+    text = raw.decode('utf-8')
+    if not text.strip():
+        raise ValueError('Annotation guide must not be blank')

+def load_selection(snapshot_dir, selection_dir, eligibility_cutoff=None):
+    """Verify saved eligibility evidence; never reuse a judgment for changed text."""
+    rows, manifest, dataset_hash, manifest_hash = load_snapshot(snapshot_dir)
+    folder = Path(selection_dir)
+    report_raw = (folder/'selection.json').read_bytes()
+    report = json.loads(report_raw)
+    if report.get('kind') != 'development_eligibility_selection_v1':
+        raise ValueError('A saved development eligibility selection is required')
+    if (report['input_dataset_sha256'], report['input_manifest_sha256']) != (dataset_hash, manifest_hash):
+        raise ValueError('Eligibility selection refers to another snapshot')
+    for filename, key in [('eligible.jsonl', 'eligible_rows_sha256'),
+                          ('decisions.jsonl', 'decision_history_sha256'), ('policy.md', 'policy_sha256')]:
+        if hashlib.sha256((folder/filename).read_bytes()).hexdigest() != report[key]:
+            raise ValueError('Eligibility selection evidence hash mismatch')
+    decisions = [json.loads(line) for line in (folder/'decisions.jsonl').read_bytes().splitlines()]
+    if any(d['policy_sha256'] != report['policy_sha256'] or
+           utc(d['decision_available_at']) >= utc(report['cutoff']) for d in decisions):
+        raise ValueError('Eligibility history does not match its policy/cutoff')
+    selected = select_eligibility(rows, decisions, report['cutoff'])
+    if selected != report['versions']:
+        raise ValueError('Eligibility decisions do not reproduce the saved selection')
+    included = {s['version_id'] for s in selected if s['status'] == 'included'}
+    eligible = [r for r in rows if r['version_id'] in included]
+    saved = [json.loads(line) for line in (folder/'eligible.jsonl').read_bytes().splitlines()]
+    if saved != eligible:
+        raise ValueError('Eligible rows differ from their exact snapshot versions')
+    if eligibility_cutoff is not None:
+        earlier = select_eligibility(rows, decisions, eligibility_cutoff)
+        included = {s['version_id'] for s in earlier if s['status'] == 'included'}
+        eligible = [r for r in rows if r['version_id'] in included]
+    return eligible, manifest, dataset_hash, hashlib.sha256(report_raw).hexdigest()

+def _seal(conn, decision_ids, kind):
+    """Clock is sampled after the decision-content transaction committed.
+
+    Release markers commit separately. Missing markers prohibit selection;
+    recovery uses its present clock, never an earlier recorded_at timestamp.
+    """
+    conn.execute('BEGIN IMMEDIATE')
+    try:
+        stamp = datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat()
+        result = []
+        for decision_id in decision_ids:
+            row = conn.execute('SELECT * FROM annotation_decisions WHERE decision_id=?',
+                               (decision_id,)).fetchone()
+            if row is None:
+                raise ValueError('Unknown annotation decision')
+            existing = conn.execute('SELECT * FROM annotation_releases WHERE decision_id=?',
+                                    (decision_id,)).fetchone()
+            if existing:
+                continue
+            if utc(stamp) < max(utc(row['recorded_at']), utc(row['data_available_at'])):
+                raise ValueError('Release clock precedes recorded judgment/data availability')
+            prior = conn.execute('''SELECT r.label_available_at FROM annotation_decisions d
+                JOIN annotation_releases r USING(decision_id)
+                WHERE d.version_id=? AND d.guide_sha256=? AND d.annotator=? AND d.sequence<?
+                ORDER BY d.sequence DESC LIMIT 1''',
+                (row['version_id'], row['guide_sha256'], row['annotator'], row['sequence'])).fetchone()
+            if prior and utc(stamp) <= utc(prior[0]):
+                raise ValueError('Release clock did not advance')
+            conn.execute('INSERT INTO annotation_releases VALUES (?,?,?)', (decision_id, stamp, kind))
+            result.append({'decision_id': decision_id, 'version_id': row['version_id'],
+                           'label_available_at': stamp, 'release_kind': kind})
+        conn.commit()

+"""Review frozen headline data and collector attempts without opening live DuckDB."""
+import argparse

+def build_review(rows, evidence):
+    """Summarize attempts within each run; never interpret repeated counts as news."""
+    links = verify_request_links(rows, evidence)
+    sessions = {s['run_id']: s for s in evidence['sessions']}
+    requests = {r['request_id']: r for r in evidence['requests']}
+    groups = defaultdict(list)
+    for request in evidence['requests']:
+        if request['run_id'] not in sessions:
+            raise ValueError('Request has no session evidence')
+        groups[(request['run_id'], request['endpoint'])].append(request)
+    source_results = []
+    for (run_id, endpoint), attempts in sorted(groups.items()):
+        attempts = sorted(attempts, key=lambda r: (utc(r['started_at']), r['request_id']))
+        starts = [utc(r['started_at']) for r in attempts]
+        intervals = [(b-a).total_seconds() for a, b in zip(starts, starts[1:])]
+        source_results.append({
+            'run_id': run_id, 'endpoint': endpoint,
+            'session_status': sessions[run_id]['status'],
+            'invalidated_run': sessions[run_id]['invalidated'],
+            'attempts': len(attempts), 'outcomes': dict(Counter(r['outcome'] for r in attempts)),
+            'http_statuses': dict(Counter(str(r['http_status']) if r['http_status'] is not None
+                                         else 'unknown' for r in attempts)),
+            'reported_items_across_attempts': sum(r['item_count'] or 0 for r in attempts),
+            'attempts_without_item_count': sum(r['item_count'] is None for r in attempts),
+            'first_attempt_started_at': attempts[0]['started_at'],
+            'last_attempt_started_at': attempts[-1]['started_at'],
+            'max_interval_between_attempt_starts_seconds': max(intervals) if intervals else None,
+            'errors': sorted({r['error'] for r in attempts if r['error']}),
+        })
+    lags = defaultdict(list)
+    for row in rows:
+        snapshot = json.loads(row['snapshot'])
+        context = snapshot.get('request_context')
+        if not context or not row.get('source_time'):
+            continue
+        # Updated/feed-created/GDELT discovery times must not be called publication.
+        kind = snapshot['source_time_kind']
+        endpoint = requests[context['request_id']]['endpoint']
+        basis = 'publication' if kind.startswith('feed:published') else kind
+        lags[(endpoint, basis)].append((utc(row['observed_at'])-utc(row['source_time'])).total_seconds()/3600)
+    distributions = [
+        {'endpoint': endpoint, 'source_time_basis': basis, 'selected_versions': len(values),
+         'min_hours': min(values), 'median_hours': statistics.median(values),
+         'max_hours': max(values), 'negative_lag_versions': sum(v < 0 for v in values)}
+        for (endpoint, basis), values in sorted(lags.items())]
+    return {'kind': 'source_quality_review_v2',
+            'database_opened': False, 'selected_versions': len(rows),
+            'selected_article_ids': len({r['article_id'] for r in rows}),
+            'request_links': links, 'source_requests_by_run': source_results,
+            'source_to_observation_lags': distributions,
+            'limitations': [
+                'Returned/selected items across repeated attempts are not unique headlines or independent events.',
+                'A successful HTTP request does not establish fresh news or successful downstream delivery.',
+                'Invalidated attempts remain debugging evidence and are identified separately.',
+                'Intervals describe attempt starts within one run, not verified continuous coverage or outage duration.',
+                'Lag distributions describe only the selected snapshot, not all feed items.',
+                'Publication, updated and GDELT discovery times are different source-time bases.',
+                'This review does not certify a drain, final eligibility, annotation quality or paper readiness.']}
+
+
+def review_sources(snapshot_dir, ledger_path, output_dir):
+    snapshot = Path(snapshot_dir)
+    manifest_bytes = (snapshot/'manifest.json').read_bytes()
+    manifest = json.loads(manifest_bytes)
+    data = (snapshot/'dataset.jsonl').read_bytes()
+    digest = hashlib.sha256(data).hexdigest()
+    if digest != manifest.get('dataset_sha256'):
+        raise ValueError('Frozen dataset hash mismatch')
+    rows = [json.loads(line) for line in data.splitlines()]
+    if len(rows) != manifest.get('rows') or len({r['version_id'] for r in rows}) != len(rows):
+        raise ValueError('Frozen dataset count or unique-version identity mismatch')
+    if (snapshot/'INVALID_FOR_RESEARCH.json').exists():
+        raise ValueError('Use a valid corrected snapshot for selected headline quality')
+    evidence = read_session_evidence(ledger_path)
+    report = build_review(rows, evidence)
+    serialized = canonical_json(evidence) + '\n'
+    report.update({'reviewed_at_utc': datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(),
+                   'snapshot_directory': str(snapshot), 'dataset_sha256': digest,
+                   'snapshot_manifest_sha256': hashlib.sha256(manifest_bytes).hexdigest(),
+                   'ledger_sha256': hashlib.sha256(serialized.encode()).hexdigest(),
+                   'reviewer_code': code_identity()})
+    output = Path(output_dir)
+    output.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=False)
+    (output/'collection-sessions.json').write_text(serialized, encoding='utf-8', newline='\n')
+    (output/'report.json').write_text(json.dumps(report, indent=2)+'\n', encoding='utf-8', newline='\n')
+    return report
+
+
+def main():
+    parser = argparse.ArgumentParser(description=__doc__)
+    parser.add_argument('--snapshot-dir', required=True)
+    parser.add_argument('--ledger', default=OUTBOX_PATH)
+    parser.add_argument('--output-dir', required=True)
+    args = parser.parse_args()
+    report = review_sources(args.snapshot_dir, args.ledger, args.output_dir)
+    print(json.dumps({key: report[key] for key in ('reviewed_at_utc', 'selected_versions',
+          'request_links', 'source_requests_by_run', 'source_to_observation_lags')}, indent=2))

+if __name__ == '__main__':
+    main()

+def row(title='Company reports higher earnings', article_id='article'):

+            'title': title, 'source': 'RSS', 'url': obs.url, 'observed_at': T.isoformat(),

+
+def review(r, **kwargs):
+    return {'version_id': r['version_id'], 'content_hash': r['content_hash'], 'english': 'yes',
